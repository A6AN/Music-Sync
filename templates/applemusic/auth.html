How to get your tokens:
  1. Open Apple Music Web Player and log in.
  2. Open Developer Tools (F12 or Right Click -> Inspect).
  3. Go to the Network tab.
  4. Play any song or browse an album.
  5. Look for a request to api.music.apple.com (e.g., search, storefronts).
  6. Click on the request and look at the Request Headers.
  7. Copy the value of Authorization (starts with Bearer ...) -> paste into Developer Token.
  8. Copy the value of Music-User-Token (starts with As... or similar) -> paste into Music User Token.
